## Step 1: Accessing the LMS & Logging In

### Requirements:
- Any web-connected device: Smartphone, Tablet, Laptop, or Desktop PC.
- An up-to-date web browser: Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari.
- Your institutional email address and initial password provided by Greg & Co. ICT or your vocational training center.

### Login Steps:
1. Open your web browser and navigate to:  
   👉 **[https://lms.gregicthub.com](https://lms.gregicthub.com)**
2. Click the **"Sign In"** or **"Teacher & Trainee Studio"** button in the top right corner.
3. On the login page ([https://lms.gregicthub.com/login](https://lms.gregicthub.com/login)):
   - Enter your registered **Email Address** (e.g., `trainee@gregicthub.com`).
   - Enter your **Password**.
4. Check the **"Remember me"** box if you are on a personal device to stay signed in.
5. Click **"Log In"**.

## Step 2: Securing Your Account & Show/Hide Password Toggle

To prevent mistyped characters, especially on mobile touchscreens or complex symbols, the LMS includes a **Show / Hide Password** button:

1. When typing your password in any password input field (Login, Registration, or Password Change), look at the right edge of the field.
2. Click or tap the **Eye Icon (👁️)**:
   - **Eye open**: Unmasks the password characters into readable text so you can confirm accuracy.
   - **Eye with a slash (👁️🗨️)**: Masks the password back to dots/bullet points for privacy in public workshop areas.
3. **Security Tip:** Never share your password or let others observe your screen while the eye toggle is active in crowded spaces.

## Step 3: Personalizing Your Interface (Light & Dark Theme)

Whether working outdoors at a technical project site in bright sunlight or studying in the evening, you can switch between **Light Mode** and **Dark Mode**:

1. Locate the **Theme Toggle** button:
   - **On Desktop/Laptop:** Located in the top navigation bar next to your profile avatar (look for the Sun ☀️ or Moon 🌙 icon).
   - **On Mobile:** Open the menu hamburger icon (☰); the theme switch is situated above your profile options.
   - **On the Landing Page:** Located directly in the top right header.
2. Click or tap the icon:
   - ☀️ **Sun Icon**: Switches to **Light Mode** (clean white background, crisp high-contrast slate text).
   - 🌙 **Moon Icon**: Switches to **Dark Mode** (deep charcoal background `#020617`, reduced eye strain in low-light environments).
3. **Automatic Persistence:** The platform automatically remembers your chosen theme on your device using local storage—there is no need to reselect it every time you log in.

## Step 6: Understanding Measurable Learning Objectives

Every accredited TVET course on Greg ICT Hub LMS is mapped to **Bloom's Revised Taxonomy**:

- **Remembering & Understanding:** Recognizing technical terms, safety hazards, and tool classifications.
- **Applying & Analyzing:** Interpreting circuit diagrams, calculating load capacities, wiring distribution boards, or analyzing weld penetration.
- **Evaluating & Creating:** Troubleshooting equipment faults, designing bespoke metal assemblies, or formulating preventative maintenance schedules.

Look for the **"Learning Objectives"** block in each module to know exactly what skills you will be tested on during practical assessments!

## Step 8: Completing and Submitting Practical Assignments

Vocational mastery requires hands-on demonstration. Here is how to submit your practical evidence:

1. In the course outline or your dashboard, click on the **Assignment** title.
2. **Review the Assignment Brief:**
   - Read the assignment instructions carefully.
   - Note the **Due Date & Time**. Submissions after the deadline may be flagged as late.
   - Review the attached **Grading Rubric** to understand how marks will be allocated.
3. **Prepare Your Submission:**
   - **Text Entry:** Type your written reflections, workshop observation notes, or calculation steps directly into the submission box.
   - **File Attachments:** Upload your practical proof:
     - 📷 High-resolution photos of your completed workshop project (e.g., completed electrical conduit bend, clean fillet weld, solar inverter connection).
     - 📄 Technical reports in PDF, DOCX, or Excel format.
     - 📦 Compressed ZIP files if submitting multiple artifacts.
4. **Submit:**
   - Click the **"Submit Practical Assignment"** button.
   - A green confirmation notification will appear at the top of your screen confirming that your work has been successfully logged with a cryptographic timestamp.

## Step 9: Understanding Rubric Assessments, Grades & Feedback

Instructors at Greg ICT Hub LMS evaluate practical TVET work using multi-dimensional **Rubrics**, ensuring fair, objective, and transparent scoring:

1. After your instructor grades your assignment, your grade status changes to **Graded / Released**.
2. Click on the assignment to view your **Rubric Assessment Report**:
   - Each criterion (e.g., *Workshop Safety & PPE Compliance*, *Dimensional Accuracy*, *Circuit Functionality*, *Housekeeping*) shows the level you attained.
   - You can see exactly how many marks were earned out of the total possible marks for each criterion.
3. **Instructor Feedback:**
   - Read specific qualitative comments from your teacher.
   - If remedial workshop corrections are needed, your instructor will note them here.

## Step 12: Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

### Q1: I cannot see my courses on the dashboard.
- **Solution:** Verify with your center administrator or instructor that your email was added to the course cohort. Once enrolled, the course appears instantly on your dashboard.

### Q2: My file upload failed when submitting an assignment.
- **Solution:** Ensure your file size is under the 25 MB limit and is in an accepted format (`.pdf`, `.jpg`, `.png`, `.docx`, `.zip`). If your internet connection is slow, try reducing the photo resolution before uploading.

### Q3: How do I change between Light Mode and Dark Mode?
- **Solution:** Click the **Sun / Moon** icon in the top header. Your preference will be saved on your device automatically.

### Q4: I forgot my password. How do I get back in?
- **Solution:** On the login page, click **"Forgot your password?"**. Enter your registered email address and you will receive a secure password reset link. Alternatively, contact your Greg & Co. ICT administrator for a password reset token.

### Q5: Can I access the LMS on my mobile smartphone?
- **Solution:** Yes! Greg ICT Hub LMS is 100% mobile responsive. Open `https://lms.gregicthub.com` in your mobile Chrome or Safari browser for the complete TVET learning experience.
